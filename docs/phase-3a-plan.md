# Phase 3A Plan

Phase 3A starts the billing and payment foundation after Phase 2 pilot-readiness
scope closure.

## Status

Phase 3A implementation scope is complete. It includes invoice/payment APIs,
multi-line invoice editing, encounter charge capture, refund and void actions,
receipt/tax invoice numbering, charge templates for common fees, insurance
claim basics, and a frontend cashier tab with receipt printing.

## Current Limitations

- Claim lifecycle is intentionally internal/basic; no payer integration or EDI
  submission yet.
- Receipt/tax invoice numbering is stored and unique per clinic, but numbering
  policy is still operator-entered rather than generated by a configurable
  sequence engine.
- Auto charge capture currently covers visit and prescription charges via
  `VISIT` and `MEDICATION` charge template codes.

## Phase 3A Closure

Phase 3A can move to UAT with cashier/front desk users. The next phase should
focus on production billing policy and external integrations:

1. Configurable receipt/tax invoice sequence generation.
2. Payer/insurer integration or claim export format.
3. Procedure/lab order modules that feed charge capture.
4. Billing reports and end-of-day cashier reconciliation.
